About

I graduated from UC Santa Barbara with a B.S. in Psychological and Brain Sciences and a minor in Applied Psychology. Over roughly three years in affective psychology research, I've moved from asking whether people can sense their own heartbeat to asking whether they can sense someone else's, and what that means for closeness, empathy, and care.

I completed my honors thesis in Dr. Jonathan Schooler's META Lab at UCSB and interned in Dr. Wendy Berry Mendes's Emotion, Health, and Psychophysiology (EHP) Lab at Yale. I care about people, not just participants, and a lot of my thinking is shaped by writers like Oliver Sacks and by volunteer work with people living with dementia.

Research Interests

Interoception

How accurately we perceive signals from our own body, and how that shapes emotion.

Alteroception

Whether we can sense another person's internal physiological state, and who we're best at sensing.

Romantic Love & Empathy

How closeness changes the way we read, feel, and respond to a partner.

Dyadic Physiology

Physiological synchrony and co-regulation between people under stress and at rest.

Projects

Honors Thesis · UCSB

To Bare One's Heart

Couples, friends, and strangers completed individual and interpersonal heartbeat detection tasks while ECG was recorded with BIOPAC. People who were better at tracking their own heartbeat were also better at guessing a partner's, across every relationship type.

  • ECG
  • BIOPAC / AcqKnowledge
  • Interoception

Yale EHP Lab

Virtual Trier Social Stress Task

An online TSST for a large NIA-funded mega-study testing stress-reduction interventions. Includes remote photoplethysmography (rPPG), an n-back task, and a video-call style evaluator interface with live performance feedback.

  • TypeScript
  • Gorilla
  • rPPG
  • Stress

Yale EHP Lab

Heartbeat Attribution Task

A replication and extension of Galvez-Pol et al. (2022): participants judge which of two people a pulsing cue belongs to, using real heartbeat signals from a 43-actor stimulus set. Paired with digit span and emotion recognition blocks.

  • BVP signal QC
  • Counterbalancing
  • Gorilla

Research Proposal · EHP Mini-Symposium

Attuned Heartbeats

Does vagal flexibility predict how accurately we sense a romantic partner's physiology compared with a stranger's? A within-subjects dyadic design measuring alteroceptive accuracy and physiological synchrony during a stressor.

  • Vagal flexibility
  • RSA
  • Synchrony
